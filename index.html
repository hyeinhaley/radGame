Radioactive Package Game

How to Play

Use the keyboard numbers 0-3 to classify radioactive packages based on their readings.

  • 0: Background radiation only (TI = background, surface = background)
  • 1: Minimal radiation (TI = background, surface slightly higher)
  • 2: Low radiation (TI slightly above background, surface moderately higher)
  • 3: Higher radiation (TI significantly above background, surface much higher)

Press SPACE to start the game or play again after game over.
